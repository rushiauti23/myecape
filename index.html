Night Town

A small town that is always awake. Walk its streets, step through the lit doors, and look for seven unlit lanterns.

Walk with W A S D or the arrow keys. Drag to look around.

Night Town needs 3D graphics (WebGL), and this browser has it switched off or unavailable. Try opening the page in Chrome, Safari or Firefox on a newer device.
